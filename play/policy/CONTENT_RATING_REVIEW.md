## Questionnaire Answers
1. **Violence**: NO (App contains no violent content, depictions of blood, or gore).
2. **Sexuality & Nudity**: NO (App contains no explicit sexual content or nudity).
3. **Language**: NO (App contains no profanity or offensive language in its interface).
4. **Controlled Substances**: NO (App does not promote drugs, alcohol, or tobacco).
5. **Miscellaneous / User Interactions**:
