Turn recordings, lectures, meetings, interviews, audio files and videos into useful text — privately on your Android device.

Offline Transcriber is built for people who want transcription without sending every minute of audio to a remote transcription server. Once the required speech model is downloaded, transcription runs locally on your device.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Import audio and video for private on-device transcription
• Record lectures, meetings, interviews and voice notes
• Follow the transcript while audio or video plays
• Tap timestamps to jump to the exact moment
• Search across your transcript library
• Bookmark important moments and organize transcripts into collections
• Turn transcripts into study notes, chapters, flashcards and quizzes
• Ask grounded questions about transcripts and your local library
• Create meeting summaries, decisions, action items and open questions
• Detect different speakers locally and rename or merge speaker labels
• Edit subtitles and export SRT or VTT
• Create captioned videos with captions permanently rendered into the video
• Export transcripts and insights as TXT, Markdown, PDF or DOCX
• Create portable backups and restore your library on another Android device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your transcript processing is designed to run on your device. Offline Transcriber does not require an account and does not use a developer-operated cloud transcription backend.

Internet access can still be used for things such as downloading optional on-device models and Google Play purchase services. When you explicitly save, share or back up a file, Android sends it only to the destination you choose.

FREE CORE TRANSCRIPTION

Core audio transcription is designed to stay useful without a monthly minute allowance.

Offline Transcriber Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks advanced features such as Accurate speech models, video transcription workflows, Study and Meeting tools, speaker intelligence, advanced exports and captioned-video export. There is no recurring Pro subscription in the current version.

BUILT FOR

• Students and lectures
• Meetings and interviews
• Journalists and researchers
• Creators working with video captions
• Anyone who prefers local-first transcription and searchable notes

IMPORTANT

Transcription and speaker detection accuracy can vary with audio quality, background noise, language, accent, device performance and overlapping speech. On-device processing speed also varies by phone.
