# Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App Title
Offline Transcriber

## Short Description (max 80 chars)
Private audio and video transcription that runs on your phone.

---

## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Turn voice recordings, lectures, meetings, interviews, and videos into searchable, timestamped transcripts without uploading your private audio to the cloud.

Offline Transcriber runs advanced Whisper speech recognition directly on your Android device. Your recordings never leave your phone, keeping your conversations 100% confidential.

---

### 🎙️ KEY FEATURES

• **Unlimited On-Device Audio Transcription**
Import audio files (MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, AAC) or record live with the built-in microphone recorder. Enjoy unlimited core transcription without monthly credit limits.

• **100% Private & Works in Airplane Mode**
Once your chosen speech model is downloaded, transcription runs entirely offline. Zero cloud uploads, zero ads, and zero third-party tracking.

• **Synchronized Audio & Transcript Playback**
Follow along with interactive word-level or segment-level text. Tap any timestamp to instantly seek and listen to that exact moment.

• **Global Search & Instant Bookmarking**
Search across your entire transcript library with SQLite full-text search. Save important moments with custom notes and organize files into Collections.

• **Subtitle Studio (Pro Lifetime)**
Import videos (MP4, MKV, WebM), extract audio, view live synchronized video subtitles, edit text on the fly, and export standard SRT and VTT subtitle files.

• **Study Intelligence (Pro Lifetime)**
Transform long lectures and meeting recordings into structured Key Points, Chapters, Summary Overviews, interactive Flashcards, and multiple-choice Quizzes powered by on-device intelligence.

• **Persistent Background Queue**
Enqueue multiple audio and video files. The FIFO transcription engine processes your files sequentially in the background and automatically recovers if the app restarts.

• **Whisper Model Manager**
Choose the perfect balance of speed and precision:
- **Fast (`tiny-q5`)**: Lightning fast for quick notes and older hardware.
- **Balanced (`base-q5`)**: High accuracy for daily lectures and interviews.
- **Accurate (`small-q5`)**: Maximum precision for complex terminology and multilingual recordings.

• **No Subscriptions — One-Time Lifetime Upgrade**
Core audio transcription and search remain free and unlimited. Upgrade once with Google Play to Lifetime Pro to unlock video subtitles, study intelligence, and unlimited queues forever.

---

### 🔒 YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS
Offline Transcriber is engineered with privacy as a foundational principle:
- Audio recordings are processed on your device's CPU/GPU via whisper.cpp.
- No developer server or user login required.
- Android cloud backups are disabled for your transcript databases to prevent unintentional cloud exposure.
- Comprehensive Privacy Center allows you to wipe cache, delete transcripts, or reset data in one tap.
