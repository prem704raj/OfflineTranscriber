# Google Play Store Listing

## App Name
Offline Transcriber

## Short Description
Private audio & video transcription that runs on your phone.

## Full Description
Turn lectures, meetings, voice recordings and videos into searchable text — privately on your phone.

Offline Transcriber uses local speech recognition, so your phone performs core transcription instead of sending every recording to a transcription server.

### KEY FEATURES

• **Unlimited core audio transcription**
Import audio or record directly. Core audio transcription has no monthly minute-credit allowance.

• **Private on-device speech recognition**
Download a transcription model once, then transcribe supported audio locally, including in airplane mode.

• **Search every transcript**
Search your local transcript library and jump directly to the timestamp where a topic was mentioned.

• **Record lectures and meetings**
Record, pause, resume and create timestamped transcripts with synced playback.

• **Choose your transcription model**
Fast and Balanced are available in the free experience. Pro unlocks the Accurate model for stronger supported devices.

• **Video transcription with Pro**
Import videos, create/edit subtitles, adjust timing and export SRT/VTT.

• **Study tools with Pro**
Create key points, chapters, flashcards and quizzes with local-first study tools. Supported devices can use optional on-device AI enhancement.

• **Organize your knowledge**
Bookmarks, collections and global search help you return to important moments.

• **Background processing**
User-started transcription can continue through the foreground processing queue, subject to Android background-processing limits.

• **Android Share integration**
Send supported media into Offline Transcriber directly from Android's Sharesheet.

### PRIVACY-FIRST
Core transcription runs locally after the speech model is installed. Recordings are not sent to a developer-operated transcription server.

### NO SUBSCRIPTION
Unlimited core audio transcription stays free.
Offline Transcriber Pro is a one-time lifetime upgrade.
