# Google Play Asset Plan

## 1. App Icon
- **Specification:** 512 x 512 px, 32-bit PNG with alpha, max 1024 KB.
- **Design:** Modern indigo and emerald microphone/soundwave motif.

## 2. Feature Graphic
- **Specification:** 1024 x 500 px, JPG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).
- **Copy:** "Private transcription. On your phone."
